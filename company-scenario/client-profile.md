
# Northstar Financial Technologies — Client Profile

> Fictional organization created for a cybersecurity GRC and IT audit portfolio simulation. All company details, systems, and future audit evidence are synthetic.

## 1. Organization Overview

**Organization:** Northstar Financial Technologies  
**Industry:** Financial Technology (FinTech)  
**Location:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada  
**Employees:** 150  
**Business Model:** Cloud-based financial technology services for small and medium-sized businesses.

Northstar provides a fictional platform that allows business customers to manage payment workflows, financial reporting, and transaction-related records.

## 2. Technology Environment

| Component | Technology | Business Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Identity Management | Microsoft Entra ID | Authentication and workforce access |
| Productivity | Microsoft 365 | Email, collaboration, document management |
| Endpoints | Windows 11 | Employee workstations |
| Cloud Infrastructure | AWS | Hosting business applications |
| Source Control | GitHub | Application code and change management |
| Security Monitoring | Centralized security logging | Security event collection and investigation |
| Third-Party Services | SaaS vendors | Business operations and customer support |

These technologies represent the simulated environment and do not imply actual deployed infrastructure.

## 3. Business-Critical Assets

- Customer financial and transaction-related information
- Employee and contractor identity records
- Cloud-hosted business applications
- Source-code repositories
- Financial reporting systems
- Authentication and access management services

## 4. Business and Security Objectives

1. Protect confidential customer and business information.
2. Prevent unauthorized access to sensitive systems.
3. Improve cybersecurity governance and risk management.
4. Strengthen IT General Controls.
5. Prepare for a future SOC 2 Type II examination.
6. Assess alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and NIST CSF 2.0.
7. Establish measurable risk treatment and remediation processes.

## 5. Assessment Scope

The simulated engagement will assess:

- Governance, policies, and security responsibilities
- Cybersecurity risk management
- Identity and access management
- User provisioning and deprovisioning
- Privileged access and periodic access reviews
- IT change management
- IT operations, backup monitoring, and incident management
- Third-party risk management
- Security awareness and selected compliance controls

## 6. Assessment Frameworks

- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and relevant Annex A controls
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0
- SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria
- IT General Controls (ITGC) assessment principles

Framework mappings will distinguish requirements, guidance, and assessment criteria rather than treating the frameworks as interchangeable.

## 7. Engagement Limitations

This is a simulated assessment using fictional company information and synthetic evidence.

The project does not represent:
- An actual client engagement
- An accredited ISO 27001 certification audit
- An independent SOC 2 examination or attestation
- An authorized assessment of a real financial institution

Findings and recommendations will be based on documented assumptions and simulated testing.
